Architecture

The X Algorithm Pipeline

Every For You feed request flows through these stages. Click each stage to explore how it works. Source: xai-org/x-algorithm

🔍
Query Hydration
Fetch user engagement history & following list
▾

The pipeline begins by loading your User Action Sequence — a log of recent engagement history (likes, replies, shares) — and your account metadata (following list, preferences). This becomes the context the Grok-based Phoenix transformer uses to personalize scores.

Input
User Action Sequence
Input

⚡
Candidate Sourcing — Thunder
In-memory in-network post retrieval from followed accounts
▾

Thunder is an in-memory post store and real-time ingestion pipeline. It consumes post create/delete events from Kafka, maintains per-user stores for original posts, replies/reposts, and video posts, then serves "in-network" candidates from accounts you follow. It enables sub-millisecond lookups without hitting an external database — posts older than the retention window are automatically trimmed.

Candidate Sourcing — Phoenix Retrieval
Two-tower ML model discovers out-of-network posts via similarity search
▾

Phoenix retrieval uses a Two-Tower model to find relevant out-of-network posts from the global corpus. The user tower encodes your engagement history; the candidate tower encodes all posts. Top-K posts are retrieved via dot product similarity between the two embeddings.

Scoring — Phoenix Transformer + Weighted Scorer
Grok-based transformer predicts engagement probabilities; Weighted Scorer combines them
▾

The Phoenix ranking model uses a Grok-based transformer (ported from xAI's Grok-1) adapted for recommendation. It uses special candidate isolation — candidates cannot attend to each other, only to user context — ensuring scores are consistent and cacheable regardless of what else is in the batch.

Final Score = Σ ( weight_i × P(action_i) )
where positive actions have positive weights
and negative actions have negative weights
P(favorite)+high
P(repost)+high
P(reply)+medium
P(quote)+medium
P(click)+low
P(video_view)+medium
P(follow_author)+high
P(share)+medium
P(not_interested)−high
P(block_author)−high
P(mute_author)−medium
P(report)−extreme
⚠️ Important: The current xai-org/x-algorithm repo contains no hardcoded numeric weights. The system has eliminated all hand-engineered features. The Grok-based transformer learns weight magnitudes from engagement data. The table above shows relative direction (positive vs. negative) based on the repo's Weighted Scorer design — not exact multipliers. Any source citing specific numbers like "30x for favorites" is referencing the old 2023 twitter/the-algorithm repo, not this one.
Author Diversity Scorer runs after the Weighted Scorer to attenuate repeated author scores — ensuring no single account dominates the feed.
🏆
Selection
Sort by final score, select top K candidates
▾

Candidates are sorted by their final composite score (after all scorers). The top K candidates advance to post-selection processing. The ScoredPostsService (gRPC endpoint) in Home Mixer returns the ranked list.

PHOENIX SIMILARITY SEARCH — TREND ALIGNMENT TIPS

Phoenix retrieval finds you through embedding similarity. Here's how to make your content surface in the Out-of-Network layer.

⚡ Thunder Layer
In-Network Optimization
Post consistently — Thunder's in-memory store rewards recency with sub-millisecond lookups for followers
Engage with followers' replies to strengthen engagement history signals used during Query Hydration
Video posts get a dedicated store in Thunder — separate from text posts
🤖 Phoenix Layer
Out-of-Network Discovery
The Candidate Tower encodes your post into an embedding — rich, specific text creates more distinctive embeddings
Hash-based embeddings power both retrieval and ranking — consistent terminology helps
High engagement density (likes per impression) pushes your post embedding toward high-engagement cluster centroids
📊 Weighted Scorer
Maximize Your Score
Favorites and reposts are the highest-weight positive actions — prioritize content that triggers them
P(follow_author) carries high weight — posting content that gains follows boosts your score significantly
Never trigger P(report) — it carries an extreme negative weight, far exceeding all positive signals
🔽 Filter Avoidance
Pass the Filters
Avoid duplicate content — DropDuplicatesFilter and RepostDeduplicationFilter will remove you
No hardcoded link penalty exists in the current repo — the transformer learns from engagement patterns, not URL presence rules
Fresh content is critical — AgeFilter removes posts beyond the retention threshold
